───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CENTRAL QUESTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Does sovereignty — the structural responsibility that organizes
  authority, normative reference, and veto relations within an
  autonomous agent system — constitute an independent architectural
  dimension, or is it merely an emergent descriptive label for the
  interaction of existing dimensions?

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FOUR-LEVEL FUNCTIONAL STRATIFICATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  L1 — Psychological Decision-Making
       How decisions are generated.
  L2 — Decision Authority
       How conflicts are resolved.
  L3 — Normative Authority
       Where criteria originate.
  L4 — Sovereignty Architecture
       How levels L1–L3 are structurally related (hypothesized;
       independence undetermined).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  TESTING DICHOTOMY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  H1 — Emergent Reducibility
       Sovereignty is an emergent label; adds no independent
       structural responsibility.

  H2 — Irreducible Architectural Responsibility
       A structural responsibility exists that cannot be fully
       reconstructed from existing dimensions.
