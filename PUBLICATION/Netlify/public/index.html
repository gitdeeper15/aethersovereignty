The Architectural Independence of Sovereignty in Autonomous Agent Systems

A Falsification-First Investigation of Authority Topology, Normative Reference, and Veto Structure

Status of this Repository

Status: Phase 0 (Conceptual Genesis and Methodological Reconstruction — Complete). This is a research repository, not a software repository. No implementation is planned unless the architectural investigation supports it.

Decision: (E) undetermined (interim). No failure condition has been falsified; no observable benefit has been demonstrated.

"AetherSovereignty is a candidate, not a result."

Overview

AetherSovereignty is the fourteenth candidate of the Aether family. It investigates whether a genuine architectural gap exists between the structural responsibility that organizes authority relations and the existing dimensions — particularly AetherGovern (rules), AetherIdentity (identity), and AetherRuntime (execution).

AetherSovereignty is not a replacement for AetherGovern or AetherIdentity. All remain dimensions or candidates with their own status.

The paper (v2.0.0) is published on Zenodo: 10.5281/zenodo.24466801 and Figshare: 10.6084/m9.figshare.35091439.

Central Question

Does sovereignty — the structural responsibility that organizes authority, normative reference, and veto relations within an autonomous agent system — constitute an independent architectural dimension, or is it merely an emergent descriptive label for the interaction of existing dimensions?

Decision (Phase 4, Interim)

AetherSovereignty is classified as (E) undetermined (interim).

The evidence is insufficient to accept AetherSovereignty as an independent dimension, and insufficient to reject it.

"Undetermined" is not a failure. It states that the evidence is insufficient to decide, and names what is missing.

Independent Review (Phase 2)

Phase 2 of the investigation is in progress. The independent reviewer, Muhammad Talha (Creator of pgRAG-Inspector, Co-Lead of Evidence Sufficiency & Grounding Protocol), completed the review on 2026-11-23.

Recommendation: Confirm the current state: Phase 0 Complete, Decision (E) Undetermined (interim), Phase 2 in progress via this review.

Full review: REVIEW/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md

The review confirms that the documentation is transparent about its non-claims, open boundaries, and untested status, and that (E) Undetermined (interim) is warranted given that no failure condition is falsified yet no positive specification or empirical execution establishes independence or benefit.

Threats to Validity

Seven threats are identified and documented openly (T1–T7). The most severe are T1 (author-on-author reasoning), T2 (no independent review), T6 (no measurement), and T7 (unresolved Govern and Identity).
