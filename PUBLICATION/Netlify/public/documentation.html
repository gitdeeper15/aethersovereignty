Four-Level Functional Stratification

LevelNameGuiding Question
L1Psychological Decision-MakingHow are decisions generated?
L2Decision AuthorityHow are conflicts resolved?
L3Normative AuthorityWhere do criteria originate?
L4Sovereignty ArchitectureHow are L1–L3 structurally related? (hypothesized; independence undetermined)
The existence of these four distinctions does not constitute evidence of an independent architectural entity. They serve strictly as analytical coordinates to test a core structural dilemma.

Falsification Methodology

  1. Architectural Hypothesis — stated to be falsifiable.
  2. Conceptual Genesis and Methodological Reconstruction — bracketing, not replacement.
  3. Four-Level Stratification — analytical coordinates, not structural evidence.
  4. Testing Dichotomy — H1 (Emergent Reducibility) vs. H2 (Irreducible Architectural Responsibility).
  5. Falsification Design — AS-F1, AS-F2, AS-F5.
  6. Independence Decision — currently: (E) Undetermined, interim.
  7. Specification — only if the independence decision is positive.
  8. Implementation — only after specification.
No implementation begins before step 6. Stopping after step 5 is a legitimate outcome. So is a negative decision.
