# Conceptual Genesis

AetherSovereignty originated from a comparative philosophical investigation
into the architecture of human authority. Its initial conceptual structure
was subsequently reconstructed within the framework of Western psychology
and systems architecture.

The metaphysical premises of the original comparative formulation were
bracketed rather than rejected or replaced. The resulting research formulation
sets them aside within the defined scope of investigation, focusing instead
on testable questions concerning self-regulation, normative authority,
decision hierarchy, regulatory influence, and veto mechanisms.

Conceptual continuity does not imply epistemological continuity.
